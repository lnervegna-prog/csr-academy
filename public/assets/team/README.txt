Team photos for the Day 2 "Meet your team" slideshow.

Drop a file in here named after the person, lowercase. Any of these work:

  .webp   .jpg   .jpeg   .png

The slideshow tries each in that order and uses whichever exists, so you do
not have to convert anything.

  amanda     Amanda    - Assistant Manager
  chrystal   Chrystal  - Lead Trainer
  roxy       Roxy
  nakeemah   Nakeemah
  kel        Kel
  ashley     Ashley
  jacqui     Jacqui
  aaliyah    Aaliyah

Square images work best - they are cropped to a circle. Aim for at least
256x256 so it stays sharp on a good screen; smaller than about 150x150 will
look soft.

Until a file exists the slide shows the person's initial instead, so a missing
photo never breaks the page. Add them whenever, commit, push - that is it.
